# Autonomy cluster

Thin index for the autonomy deep dives. The hub is [AUTONOMY.md](../AUTONOMY.md):
read it first for the model and the floor. One line per file:

- [autonomy/org.md](./autonomy/org.md): the org chart, departments, who can
  block whom, and how a disagreement between peers resolves.
- [autonomy/roles.md](./autonomy/roles.md): the role index, model tiers,
  and the rules that bind every role; one charter per role under
  [autonomy/roles/](./autonomy/roles/).
- [autonomy/safety.md](./autonomy/safety.md): authorized and forbidden
  actions, the trust model, push-back, the irreversible-data gate, the
  security veto, stop conditions, escalation.
- [autonomy/composition.md](./autonomy/composition.md): the 20-slot
  budget, categories and flow rules, slot sizes and ceilings, author
  weighting, the contributor floor, mandate decay.
- [autonomy/item-classes.md](./autonomy/item-classes.md): the item
  classes, each with its deliverable and its verification standard,
  including deferred verification for org work.
- [autonomy/release-loop.md](./autonomy/release-loop.md): the seven phases
  of one release, waves, concurrency and merge discipline, the area
  rotation, the verification standard.
- [autonomy/impact.md](./autonomy/impact.md): the impact bar, who judges
  it, and what below-bar does to the next release.
- [autonomy/issue-triage.md](./autonomy/issue-triage.md): the issue loop,
  decision tree, voice and identity of replies, the follow-through feed.
- [autonomy/watchdogs.md](./autonomy/watchdogs.md): liveness checks, the
  roster contract for concurrent children, and the recovery ladder for
  stalled agents.
- [autonomy/infrastructure.md](./autonomy/infrastructure.md): outage versus
  work failure, the preflight, and build-ahead mode.
- [autonomy/visibility.md](./autonomy/visibility.md): the per-spawn run
  log and what the engagement report derives from it.
- [autonomy/souls.md](./autonomy/souls.md): the personality layer, the
  casting, and the hard bound on what a soul may influence.

Decisions that bind future releases live outside the cluster in
[adr/](./adr/README.md), one numbered record per adopted decision.
